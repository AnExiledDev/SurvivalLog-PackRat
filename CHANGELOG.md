# Changelog

## 0.5.10

- Small speed up: the mod reads the time in a cheaper way.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.21671.

## 0.5.9

- Away from home, items that no longer fit now go on the floor, the way the game drops them. Before, Pack Rat put them in shopping bags back home while you were out.
- With `UninstallPrep` on, a load while you're out no longer says Pack Rat is safe to remove. Your storage at home only gets the game's values when you're home, so the message now tells you to go home and save there first.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.21671.

## 0.5.8

- Changing a bag setting in the middle of a game now gives storage and the car trunk their own weight limit and slot count. Before, every one of them got the limit of the shopping bags by the door.
- A change made just after a load is no longer lost. It's applied once the load is done.
- With `UninstallPrep` on, the "safe to remove" message only shows once the game's values are really back.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.21671.

## 0.5.7

- With `ScrollBars` off and a wide bag, the stove screen now shrinks to fit the window. Before, the recipe book was cut off at the right edge.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.21671.

## 0.5.6

- With `ScrollBars` off, the brewing, dyeing, trap and shop screens now shrink to fit the window too. Before, a big bag ran them off the edge of the screen.
- With `ScrollBars` off, the key hints under a big bag no longer get cut off at the bottom of the window.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.21671.

## 0.5.5

- The bag and shop screens use the whole window again instead of starting below the clock, so more of your bag shows before it needs to scroll.
- The key hints under your bag stay on one line again.
- With things lying on the ground nearby, the screen moves up to make room for the strip that shows them, so the strip no longer covers the bottom of your bag.
- A screen only a little too wide for the window is now shrunk a little instead of cutting a column off your bag.
- On a small window, a screen too tall to fit is now drawn a little smaller with its grids scrolling, instead of shrinking to about half size.
- The material rack's rows now scroll, so the screen no longer gets smaller as the rack fills up.
- The trash can's pit now scrolls and opens at the bottom where the pile is, so the screen no longer gets smaller as the pile grows.
- Fixed: the key hints under a very full bag could still run off the bottom of the window.
- Fixed: on a small window the trap screen cut your bag and the storage box down to one row. Your bag now fills the window's height, and the storage box keeps all its rows.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.21671.

## 0.5.4

- Fixed: the car trunk and big storage screens shrank everything to fit. Your bag and the storage now stay full size and scroll instead.
- Fixed: the bag and the car trunk could be squeezed into thin strips. A screen that really can't fit now shrinks the way it does without Pack Rat.
- Scroll bars now sit right next to the grid instead of at the far edge of a wide panel.
- The bag and shop screens now start below the clock, so the clock no longer covers the top of them.
- The note on the car trunk screen now wraps inside the trunk panel instead of making it wider.
- The key hints under your bag now always stay on screen.
- With storage or the car trunk open next to your bag, the key hints wrap to fit under the bag, so they no longer cover the buttons at the bottom of the screen.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.21671.

## 0.5.3

- Mod Manager's Conflicts tab can now see what Pack Rat changes in the game, like item sizes and weights and how much each bag holds, so it can show when another mod changes the same things.
- Now needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.25.0 or newer.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.21271.

## 0.5.2

- Clearer wording in the settings and on screen.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.5.1

- The search boxes in the Inventories and Items tabs now find every word you type, in any order. "backpack camping" finds Camping Backpack, and a space before or after a word no longer hides everything.
- Leaving a number box blank no longer sets it to the lowest value. A blank box, or one with no number in it, goes back to the setting. The - and + buttons next to it also count from the setting.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.5.0

- New `ScrollBars` setting, on by default. Turn it off and the game shrinks the whole screen to fit a big bag, as it does without Pack Rat, which can make a very big bag too small to use.
- `ScrollBars` applies right away, even on a screen that is already open.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.4.3

- Pack Rat now needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.19.0 or newer.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.4.2

- The Inventories tab in Mod Manager no longer has a scrollbar of its own, and the bag preview is shown at full size instead of shrinking to a few pixels on some screens.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.4.1

- Pack Rat now changes nothing until you set it up: `CarryWeight` starts at 1, the game's weight limit, instead of 2. If you already play with the mod, your saved setting stays as it is.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.4.0

- Big bags now scroll on every bag screen, including the Brewing Barrel, the Cooking Station and the Juicer, and the screen looks right as soon as it opens instead of after a short flash.
- New "All items half size" switch on the Items tab: every item takes half the space, rounded down, and a long item stays long. A 3x2 item becomes 2x1 and a 2x2 item becomes 1x1. A size set for a category or a single item still comes first.
- The Inventories and Items tabs keep their place when you click a button, change a value or pick a category, instead of jumping back to the top.
- Fixed on the Inventories tab: a horizontal scrollbar that did nothing, and category buttons running past the edge of the window.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.3.0

- Inventories are now sorted into six categories of about the same size: Bags, Vehicles, Small storage, Medium storage, Large storage and Extra large storage. On the Inventories tab you can set the columns, rows or size multiplier for a whole category at once.
- A size set for one inventory still beats the size of its category. The old Bags, Vehicles and Storage multipliers carry over to the new categories.
- Changes now apply right away while you are in a game: bag and storage sizes, weight limits, item sizes and item weights. Outside a game they apply when you load a save.
- When a bag gets smaller or an item gets bigger during a game, items that no longer fit move to a free spot in the same bag. If the bag is full, they go into the shopping bags in the living room.
- Very tall or wide bags now get a scrollbar on the bag screen, instead of the whole screen shrinking to fit them.
- Fixed on the Inventories tab: the weight limit box cut off big numbers, the "default" label covered the grid preview, the first inventory under a heading was hidden, and the preview could run off the bottom of the window.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.2.0

- Bags, storage and vehicles can now get more space as well as more weight. Mod Manager has a new Inventories tab: make everything bigger at once, or set the columns, rows and weight limit of one bag or storage piece, with a preview of how a full bag looks.
- Items can now take up less space and weigh less. Mod Manager has a new Items tab: make every item 1x1, set a size or weight for a whole category, or change a single item.
- New setting `ItemWeight` makes every item lighter or heavier, from 0.1x to 3x.
- New setting `Overweight` changes the slowdown from a heavy load. Default keeps the game's slowdown, Custom lets you choose where it starts and how strong it is, and Hard limit removes the slowdown but stops you picking up more at the limit.
- When a bag gets smaller, the items that no longer fit go into the shopping bags in the living room instead of being lost. If those are full, the items go on the floor.
- New setting `UninstallPrep` puts every size and weight back to the game's before you remove the mod, and tells you when it is safe to remove.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.11.0 or newer is now required. Pack Rat uses it for its messages.
- Changes made on the Mods screen no longer need a game restart. They take effect the next time you load a save.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.1.0

- First version.
- Every bag, storage piece and vehicle can carry more weight, twice as much by default. Set it anywhere from 0.1x to 1000x.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.
